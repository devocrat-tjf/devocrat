Questions

Do I need to know how to type on one?

If you can use a keyboard, you can use a typewriter. Press firmly, return the carriage with a flick, and listen for the bell near the end of the line.

Can I buy ribbons without buying a machine?

Yes. Tell us the make and model and we’ll cut and spool the right ribbon for you.

What does shipping cost?

A flat $28 within the country, fully insured. Every machine travels in a padded wooden crate.

Can you repair a machine I didn’t buy from you?

Most makes, yes. Clean and oil starts at $45; a full restoration is $180. We always quote before starting.
